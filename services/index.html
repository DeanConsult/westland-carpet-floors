Our Flooring Services

Westland Carpet and Floors has just about any type of flooring you may need. Call us if you don’t see what you’re looking for.

Carpeting

We have been working with carpet for over 30 years. We offer carpet styles such as loop, level cut, Berber, and plush in a huge variety of colors and price ranges. Throughout our years in the industry, we’ve built strong relationships with leading manufacturers including Shaw Carpet, Kraus, and Mohawk. Wherever you need carpeting, we’re ready to install it better than anyone else.

Hardwood Floors

One of the best-looking, easiest to care for, and longest-lasting flooring options available. Our specialists can offer you a variety of styles, sizes, and colors for your hardwood floors. We advise on traffic patterns, sun exposure, and location to make sure your hardwood floors last for decades. Installing hardwood floors correctly takes experience — choose us to get it right.

Laminate Floors

Laminate floors offer a huge range of colors and styles to pick from. This customization along with the low price compared to hardwood makes laminate flooring a very popular option. Our flooring specialists will bring samples right to your home to help you find the perfect look for your space and budget.

Ceramic Tile

Timeless, durable, and easy to clean, ceramic tile is ideal for bathrooms, kitchens, entryways, and basements. We carry an extensive selection of colors, sizes, and patterns. Our installation team ensures every tile is set level and grouted perfectly for a finish that lasts.

Vinyl Tile & Linoleum

Custom vinyl tile and linoleum provide excellent durability, comfort underfoot, and easy maintenance. Available in a wide range of patterns including wood-look and stone-look styles. An affordable and practical choice for kitchens, basements, laundry rooms, and high-traffic areas.

Outdoor Carpeting

We supply and install outdoor carpeting for patios, decks, sunrooms, stairs, and more. Outdoor carpet is designed to withstand weather, UV exposure, and heavy foot traffic while still looking great. Perfect for garages, screened porches, and commercial entryways.

Commercial Flooring

We are just as comfortable installing flooring in commercial locations as residential ones. Over 30 years in the business has equipped us to handle the intricacies of commercial projects. We’ve installed flooring in dental offices, warehouses, garages, restaurants, and more. Evening and weekend appointments help minimize disruption to your operations.
